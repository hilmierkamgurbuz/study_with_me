D-012: Face-atlas cell alignment is corrected **in the texture**, not with a per-cell offset table in code or a data asset. The 16 cells were unregistered — eye-line centre ran 0.507 on row 1 but 0.353 on row 4, so Idle(`Neutral` r1c1) → Listening(`Caring` r4c3) jumped the face up 0.147 of a cell — and each cell's pixels were translated by whole pixels onto one shared anchor (eye-line spread now 0.0010, the integer-rounding floor; 0 pixels lost, no resampling). Anchor is the bbox centre of every connected component **except** the mouth, because open-mouth cells (Laugh, Yawn, TalkWide) extend downward and a whole-content anchor would shift them the wrong way; validated independently by mouth-top convergence (0.223 spread → 0.02 on closed-mouth cells). Rationale: `CharacterPresenter`'s 4x4 grid math, `faceZoom` and the material's baked tiling all stay valid because cell boundaries never move, and no second authority over cell geometry is created. Not fixable this way: per-cell feature *scale* variance (Caring's eyes are larger than Neutral's) — that needs art regeneration — affects: Assets/Art/Textures/Chloe_FacialFeatures_Only_4x4_2560x2048_Transparent.png, Assets/deneme material/Chloe_Faces_Alpha.mat — 2026-08-12
D-013: Mixamo clips are consumed as tool-generated `.anim` assets (`Assets/Art/chloe/Generated/`, written only by `ChloeClipPathFixer`), not as FBX sub-assets. Cause: 29 of the 30 `Chloe_Rigged@*.fbx` files hold a single scene-root node, so Unity makes that node the imported root and their curve paths come out as `spine/...`, while `deneme.fbx` has three root nodes and uses `Armature/spine/...`. Nothing binds, and `m_WriteDefaultValues: 1` then snaps the rig to its standing rest pose — the "she stands up when talking" bug. `Sitting Idle` is the only 3-root clip, which is why it alone worked. The tool resolves each path against the model's real hierarchy (unambiguous suffix match only, unresolved paths reported not guessed), sets `loopTime` so Loop Time needs no import-setting change, and drops root-motion curves for **all** clips — the baked seated offsets disagree by 8 cm Y / 23 cm Z, so stripping only one would make the lurch worse. Consequence: clips are strictly in-place, the rig sits at the model's rest origin, and Chloe's X/Z is positioned once in the scene (head Y self-corrects via the head-height pin) — affects: Assets/Editor/ChloeClipPathFixer.cs, Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeController.controller, Assets/Art/chloe/Generated/ — 2026-08-12
D-014: Both `ChloeController` transitions had `m_HasExitTime: 1` with exit times 0.968/0.994, so a state change waited until the clip nearly ended — 7.6 s out of the 7.87 s idle, 44.7 s out of the 44.97 s talking clip — and the body never followed the voice. Cleared on both, blend shortened 0.25 → 0.2 s, so `IsTalking` takes effect immediately. Side effect worth knowing: this makes D-013's rest-pose snap visible the instant speech starts rather than ~45 s later, which is why the two land together — affects: Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeController.controller — 2026-08-12
D-015: Removed `CharacterPresenter`'s head-height pin (`headBone` / `targetHeadHeight`, `pos.y += targetHeadHeight - headBone.position.y`) and locked Y alongside X/Z to the Start-captured pose. The pin fixed the *head* at a constant world height, which means the *body* is what moved to satisfy it — harmless while the Talking clip drove nothing, but once D-013 made that clip actually animate, its forward-leaning pose pushed her hips off the chair. Its original cause, Mixamo root position curves, is gone: D-013 strips root-motion curves at generation and both clips now share one Armature placement. Locking Y is also what makes the new offset safe — nudging a live `transform.position` each frame would accumulate. Added in its place: `speakingPositionOffset`, `speakingRotationOffset` and `poseBlendSeconds`, applied in her resting frame only while speaking and blended in/out, because the `Sitting Talking` clip's own pose leans her forward far enough that the room camera sees the top of her head instead of her face and a clip's pose cannot be edited from the component. Values are serialized Inspector fields, so the framing lives in the scene, not in code — the defaults are an untested starting point. If a future clip carries genuine root motion, the fix is stripping it at import, not reinstating the pin — affects: Assets/Scripts/Presentation/CharacterPresenter.cs, Assets/Prefabs/Chloe.prefab, Assets/Scenes/Room.unity — 2026-08-12
D-016: `Chloe_Face` is now rigid-bound: one vertex group, 100% `spine.005` (the head bone), replacing the automatic-weight split of 52.7% `spine.005` / 46.7% `spine.004` (neck) plus strays on `spine.006` and both shoulders. That split made the decal follow only half of every head movement, so the eyes, brows and mouth slid across the face — a flat decal must not deform. Re-authored in Blender over the MCP connection and exported over `deneme.fbx`. The round-trip was the risky part and was measured, not assumed: the exported file went to a scratch path first and was diffed against the live asset before replacing it. Two non-obvious traps found that way. (a) Blender's importer parents skinned meshes to the armature, but this FBX has `Armature`, `Chloe` and `Chloe_Face` as scene-root siblings — exporting the parented form would have left a single root node, and Unity then makes that node the imported root, stripping the `Armature/` prefix every generated clip binds through (the D-013 failure, reintroduced on both clips). Parents were cleared, keeping transforms; skinning is unaffected because it comes from the Armature modifier. (b) `add_leaf_bones` must be OFF, not ON as first assumed: the 11 `*_end` bones import as real bones, so adding leaf bones again appends a second tier. Verified identical after export: 3 scene-root nodes, 45 transform nodes, 2 geometries, every hierarchy path, all 7 material names (so the importer's externalObjects remap re-binds), and the face UV bounds to six decimals — the last one is what keeps the material's baked cell tiling and `faceZoom` valid — affects: Assets/Art/Character/deneme.fbx, Assets/Prefabs/Chloe.prefab — 2026-08-13
D-017: **Supersedes D-016 — its premise was measured wrong and the change was reverted.** D-016 rebound `Chloe_Face` to 100% `spine.005`, assuming the 52.7/46.7 head/neck split was what made the features slide across the face. Measuring the surface the decal sits on says otherwise: the body mesh's `spine.005` weight under the decal is not uniform, it runs 0.126 to 0.866 (median 0.519), so the head surface itself deforms rather than rotating rigidly — and the decal's original weights tracked that surface per-vertex almost exactly (median |delta| 0.021, 90th percentile 0.049, only 2 of 207 vertices off by more than 0.10). Binding the decal rigidly decoupled it from a surface that still moves, so it sank into the head during play. The model was restored byte-for-byte from the copy taken immediately before the overwrite, so no re-export variance entered; Blender's scene was restored to the same weights (per-vertex max difference 0.0). The lesson worth keeping: before re-weighting a decal, measure the weights of the surface it lies on — an average that looks wrong can be a per-vertex match, and 'rigid' is only correct when the thing underneath is rigid too. The original sliding complaint is attributed to the atlas instead (D-012 and the shared eye/brow pass): row 4 sat 0.147 of a cell higher than row 1, cx drifted 0.07 across the talk cycle, and the iris size changed per cell — all three fixed in the texture without touching the rig — affects: Assets/Art/Character/deneme.fbx — 2026-08-13
D-018: Chloe stays **Generic**; the leg problem is solved with an Avatar Mask + a second Animator layer instead of a Humanoid migration. Measured first: between `Sitting Idle.anim` and `Sitting Talking.anim` the first-frame pose differs by 33.1°/34.8° at `thigh.L/R`, 26.3° at `shin.R`, ~26° at both feet — so every `IsTalking` flip re-planted the legs of a seated character — while *within* the talking clip those same bones move only 1.3–7.1°, i.e. the legs are near-static content worth nothing to keep. Hips (`spine`) differ by 0.0° and move 0.0°, confirming D-013's root-motion strip holds; the forward lean D-015 offsets is in `spine.002`+`spine.003` (16.7° each), **not** the legs, so `speakingPositionOffset` is unaffected and stays. The gesture content is entirely in the arms (`upper_arm` 49–52°, `forearm` 40–68°, `hand` 100–112° of in-clip travel), all of which the mask keeps. Setup: `ChloeUpperBody.mask` lists all 46 of the model's transform paths (read from `ModelImporter.transformPaths`, not guessed) with the two `thigh.*` subtrees — 14 paths including `heel.02.*`/`toe.*` and their `_end` leaves — at weight 0; Base Layer plays `Sitting Idle` unconditionally and is the sole writer of the legs; the pre-existing two-state machine moved intact to a masked Override "Upper Body" layer (same state machine object, so the `IsTalking` transitions, 0.9 s fixed duration and `HasExitTime: 0` from D-014 were carried, not rebuilt). `m_WriteDefaultValues` set to 0 on all three states — WD ON is unpredictable under a partial mask and was the exact mechanism of D-013's snap-to-standing-rest-pose. Humanoid was rejected on cost, not feasibility: all 15 required bones exist (`spine`=Hips, `spine.001..003`, `spine.004`=Neck, `spine.005`=Head, `upper_arm/forearm/hand`, `thigh/shin/foot`) so it *would* map, but hip height under humanoid retargeting is recomputed from leg length against a stylized rig — reopening the float-above-the-chair class D-015 had just closed — the Rigify names (`spine` as the hips) would need manual avatar assignment, and flipping `deneme.fbx` risks the imported root structure that `Chloe.prefab` is a variant of (D-016's trap). Revisit only if the clip count in use grows: 31 clip FBXs exist, **2** are wired — affects: Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeUpperBody.mask, Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeController.controller — 2026-08-13
D-019: **Narrows D-018's mask boundary from "everything but the legs" to "the two arm chains only"** — user's call, and it makes the D-015 root-transform compensation unnecessary rather than merely smaller. `ChloeArmsOnly.mask` (renamed from `ChloeUpperBody.mask`; rename preserves the guid so the controller reference held) now has 12 of 46 transforms at weight 1 — `shoulder.*/upper_arm.*/forearm.*/hand.*/hand.*.001(+_end)` on both sides — and 34 at weight 0, so spine, neck, head, hips, legs and both meshes all come from the Base layer's `Sitting Idle`. Layer renamed `Upper Body` -> `Arms`, base state -> `Sitting Idle Body`. The risk that had to be cleared first: the talking clip's arm poses were authored against its own chest, so freezing the chest at idle could have swung the hands. Measured by sampling both clips on a throwaway model instance at 7 times across the 44.97 s clip and compositing the masked result by hand (idle pose + talking arm locals): hand-to-head distance moved at most 1.1 cm on the right and 2.0 cm on the left versus the authored pose. The gesture survives intact. Reason it works: the two clips' chest tilt is nearly identical (~1-3 deg apart); the difference that hides her face accumulates down the whole chain (spine.001 5.4 + spine.002 16.7 + spine.003 16.7 + spine.004 4.4 + spine.005 4.4), leaving the head ~43 deg more pitched down in Talking than in Idle. Taking arms only drops that entire accumulation while keeping the part of the clip that carries the performance (in-clip travel: upper_arm 49-52 deg, forearm 40-68 deg, hand 100-112 deg). Consequence: `speakingPositionOffset` (0.098, 0.243, 0.511) and `speakingRotationOffset` (-56.9, 23.5, -4.7) should go to zero — they were compensating for a lean that no longer reaches the head. The camera was deliberately NOT touched: it sits at 1.267 y with the head at 1.290 y but pitched +12 deg down when the geometry wants -1.2 deg, which is the real cause of the top-of-head framing and remains open by user preference. `CharacterPresenter` and the prefab were not modified — affects: Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeArmsOnly.mask, Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeController.controller — 2026-08-13
D-020: Cat/dog ambient roaming lands as `PetRoamer` on an **agent-parent**, not on the animated model. Forced by a measured constraint: the PolyOne clips carry 3 position + 4 rotation curves on the EMPTY path with first key (0,0,0), so every frame they write the animated object's own transform back to the origin - and, missed on the first pass and caught by the user, 3 scale curves keyed to (1,1,1) as well, which silently inflated cat and dog to ~3.5x and ~2.5x on Play while position/rotation failed loudly. The agent-parent therefore carries the scale too (0.2825334 cat / 0.3989600 dog) with the model left at scale 1; world scale verified unchanged across the move. Chloe is immune and it was checked, not assumed: her generated clips carry zero root position, rotation and scale curves - which is why both animals teleported out of the room in Play mode, a pre-existing bug unrelated to Chloe's mask work. `Apply Root Motion` cannot suppress it: both models import as Generic with no Root node (`rootNode=''`) and no Avatar asset exists under PolyOne, so Unity never classifies those curves as root motion in the first place and just writes them as ordinary transform curves. Resolution via ownership.md: movement and animation get separate transforms. Empty `Cat`/`Dog` parents (scale 1) hold `PetRoamer` and the world placement; the untouched vendor models sit underneath pinned to local zero, which is where the clip insists on putting them anyway. Reparenting preserved world transform exactly - position delta 0.000000 m, rotation delta 0.0000 deg, scale unchanged - verified before/after rather than assumed. Measured first: all four clips' root ROTATION first key is identity, so the parent's yaw passes through to the animal with no constant offset. Route is `PetStop[] {Vector3 position; float stayMinutes}` serialized on the component: these are world coordinates specific to `Room.unity` and would be meaningless in a ScriptableObject, same reasoning as D-015's 'framing lives in the scene'. Animator states are driven by NAME via `CrossFadeInFixedTime`, because the vendor controllers have zero parameters and zero transitions - two loose states each - and the names differ per animal (`A_CartoonAnimal-Cat__Run` has two underscores, the dog's has one); the names are Inspector fields and `Start()` validates them with `Animator.HasState`, since a wrong name makes CrossFade a silent no-op. Fixed-time rather than normalized because idle is 1.33 s and run 0.33 s. Deliberately out of scope: obstacle avoidance. Travel is a straight line, no NavMesh - stops must be authored clear of furniture. `runSpeed` default 1.2 is an eye-tuned guess; stride length is underivable because the run clip's root motion is zeroed. Extended in the same task with per-stop facing (`setFacing` + `facingYaw` on PetStop), because travel direction alone cannot express 'sit facing the bed': it is opt-in so the pre-existing behaviour (keep the heading you arrived on) stays the default, the turn runs alongside the wait clock rather than gating it so stayMinutes keeps its meaning, and a yellow gizmo stub draws the authored facing so a yaw can be typed and checked without entering Play. Adding fields to the serialized struct preserved existing scene data - verified, position/stayMinutes intact and the new fields defaulted. Vendor assets were not modified at all (controllers, clips, FBX import settings, materials), and no room dressing was moved (D-007) - affects: Assets/Scripts/Presentation/PetRoamer.cs, Assets/Scenes/Room.unity - 2026-08-13
D-021: The four PolyOne cat/dog clips ship with `loopTime: false`, so Idle froze on its last frame after 1.33 s and Run after 0.33 s - the pet would have slid to its next stop frozen mid-stride. D-022: Dance stage 1 - the three Mixamo dance takes become bound `.anim` assets and `ChloeController` gains full-body dance states. A trap found before running the tool: all three FBXs name their single take `mixamo.com`, and ChloeClipPathFixer writes output as `<clip name>.anim`, so all three would have collapsed onto `Generated/mixamo.com.anim` with only the last surviving. Fixed at the source by renaming each take to its file name in the FBX import settings, rather than by touching the tool - the names are now readable in the Animator too. Generation itself was clean: 80 of 80 non-root curves resolved unambiguously against deneme.fbx for each clip, 0 unresolved, 7 root-motion curves dropped per clip (so the dance is strictly in-place, consistent with D-013), loopTime on. Controller shape: parameters `IsDancing` (bool) + `DanceClip` (int 0-2); each dance state is entered from AnyState on `IsDancing && DanceClip==n` (0.35 s, no exit time, canTransitionToSelf off) and leaves to the layer's idle state on `!IsDancing` (0.40 s). The `IsTalking` flow was verified untouched afterwards. **The states are duplicated onto BOTH layers, playing the same clip.** A full-body dance needs the masked `Arms` layer out of the way, which normally means `SetLayerWeight` - i.e. code, which stage 1 deliberately has none of. Giving both layers the same clip on the same frame with the same durations makes the mask boundary a no-op during the dance instead. Known risk: if the layers ever fall out of phase the arms play a different moment of the dance than the body; watch for it on screen, and the fix is to zero the Arms weight from the stage-2 component, which removes the duplication. Expected and correct stage-1 appearance: she dances standing AT THE DESK, clipping through the chair - `CharacterPresenter.lockPose` still pins her root to the seated placement and nothing has moved her to the dance floor yet. Camera switching, lights, disco ball, particles, music and the UI button are stage 2/3 - affects: Assets/Art/chloe/Generated/Tut Hip Hop Dance.anim, .../Wave Hip Hop Dance.anim, .../Wave Hip Hop Dance (1).anim, Assets/Art/chloe/ChloeController.controller, Assets/Art/chloe/*.fbx import settings - 2026-08-13
D-023: Dance stage 2 - `DanceModeController` runs the whole sequence off one clock and a Phase enum: camera swap + teleport + standing idle at t=0, room lights out at t=2, disco rig + music + particles at t=3, she starts dancing at t=4, 120 s of dancing, then music and disco stop on the SAME frame while she blends back to standing idle over 1 s, and one second later the room lights, the camera and the teleport home all land together so the jump back to the desk is never on screen. Every interval is a serialized field. Three constraints shaped the code more than the spec did. (a) **Ownership:** `CharacterPresenter.LateUpdate` rebuilds Chloe's root every frame from its own captured pose, so moving her had to extend that authority rather than bypass it - `SetRestingPose(position, rotation)` plus `RestingPosition`/`RestingRotation` readbacks were added, and DanceModeController never touches `chloe.transform`. A direct write would have been reverted on the same frame AND created the second root-transform writer the component exists to prevent. (b) **Fixture grouping:** colours are assigned per FIXTURE, and each fixture's two Lights and its 'r' lamp shell are read out of its own children at Start rather than wired as flat arrays. Two hand-ordered arrays of different lengths (8 lights, 4 lamps) could not have guaranteed that a lamp glows its own light's colour, which is exactly what the request asked for. (c) **The lamp materials are assets.** `disco lamp 1..4` are shared material assets, so a runtime write to `sharedMaterial` would edit them on disk permanently; emission is driven through a MaterialPropertyBlock instead. Known side effect: they still look lit in the Editor, because their asset emission stays on and only Play overrides it. A standing idle was required and is not in the original request: her only idle was `Sitting Idle`, which on the dance floor reads as sitting in mid-air. `Chloe_Rigged@Idle.fbx` (16.63 s) was generated through ChloeClipPathFixer and added as a `Standing Idle` state on both layers behind a new `IsStanding` bool; dance exits now land there instead of on the seated idle, and idle->dance blends were widened 0.35 s -> 1.0 s per the spec. Room lights are switched via `Light.enabled`, never `GameObject.SetActive`, so the lamp meshes stay visible while dark. Start() defines the resting state itself (disco off, room lights on, dance camera off, particles stopped) rather than trusting the scene, which shipped with both cameras enabled - `Camera_dance` at depth 0 over Main Camera's -1, so the dance camera was what Game view actually showed. Also folded in: `"Trip Hop Music"` added to `check_blueprint.py`'s `IGNORED_TOP`.
